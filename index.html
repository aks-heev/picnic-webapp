Before you book

What people ask us on WhatsApp.

Anything else, message us — a person replies.

What exactly is included?+

The setup, the decor items listed on the package, our team's setup and clear-up, and the venue slot. Each package lists everything; higher packages include everything in the ones below them.

Is food included?+

Not by default. You order from the café's menu and pay the café. Some packages come with a food option, and some venues include a food voucher — it shows on the venue page.

Can I pay on arrival?+

A 30% advance holds your slot — we buy flowers and block the venue against it. The remaining amount is paid on the day.

Can I change the setup?+

Yes — colours, the message on the board, add-ons. Tell us in the booking notes or on WhatsApp after you book.

What if I need to cancel or move the date?+

Postponing keeps your advance as credit, and that credit does not expire. A cancellation forfeits the advance. Full policy

What if it rains?+

We shift your setup indoors or under shade at the same venue — same slot, same price.
