## 1. What the data actually says

**28 days, 2026-08-11 → 09-05:** 39 clicks · 1,182 impressions · 3.3% CTR · avg position 12.2
**Prior 28 days (07-14 → 08-10):** 48 · 1,421 · 3.38% · 10.8

The headline reads −19% clicks. **It is not a ranking loss.** Intersecting the 38 queries present in both periods: 21c/741i → 14c/367i with impression-weighted position flat (9.3 → 9.7). Rankings held; impressions drained as the partner-venue impression flood receded (683i → 335i). This is Trap 1 from `seo-gsc-baseline` running in reverse.

### The finding that reframes everything

Over **90 days**, non-brand non-venue queries returned **217 impressions and 5 clicks** — while the site held these positions:

| Query | Position | 90d impressions |
|---|---|---|
| outdoor birthday celebration ideas | 2.0 | 2 |
| picnic setup | 3.2 | 4 |
| picnic experience | 4.2 | 5 |
| picnic party | 4.5 | 4 |
| picnic planning | 6.8 | 4 |
| picnic company | 7.1 | 16 |
| birthday picnic setup | 4.0 | 5 |

Ranking #3 for "picnic setup" bought 4 impressions in a quarter. **This is a demand ceiling, not a ranking problem, and content cannot fix it.**

`seo-gsc-baseline`'s standing read said GSC cannot measure latent demand. That is true in general, but ranking top-5 across a dozen category terms and collecting ~200 impressions is about as close to a measurement as GSC can produce.

## 2. Why there is no Phase 0 demand test

An earlier draft of this plan gated everything on a keyword-volume test. That test was dropped because no affordable instrument can run it:

- **Google Keyword Planner** — the documented "Switch to Expert Mode → Create an account without a campaign" workaround **no longer exists**. Verified in-browser 2026-09-08: `New Google Ads account` leads straight to "Create your first campaign" with a single button and no expert-mode link anywhere in the page's interactive element tree. Navigating directly to `/aw/keywordplanner/home` redirects back into signup. A shell account (203-095-0886) now exists on aksh.eeev@gmail.com with no campaign and no billing.
- Even if reachable, free Keyword Planner returns **buckets** (10–100, 100–1K, 1K–10K), not numbers — so any gate phrased as a precise threshold is unmeasurable. See `seo-demand-test-method`.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** is free but reports Bing-only volume, and Bing holds ~1.2% of Indian search. Its documented failure mode is returning no data for geo-targeted niche terms — indistinguishable from "no demand". It cannot answer this question in this market.
- **DataForSEO** would work (exact Google data, pay-per-call) but costs money to answer a question the 90-day evidence already answers directionally.

**Decision: skip the test.** If the answer would not change the spend, buying the answer is waste. Revisit only if traffic or ambition changes materially.

## 3. Phase A — the plan

Everything below was verified against live state on 2026-09-08. Items dropped after verification are recorded in §5 so they are not re-proposed.

### Tier 1 — Live bugs. No judgment required.

**A1. Fix the two broken TerraCottage meta descriptions.** 🔴
- `/venues/terracottage-sienna` serves the literal string `[PLACEHOLDER — set real copy` to Google
- `/venues/terracottage-umber` describes *"The Gathering is a cozy 2BHK apartment…"* — wrong property, stale from a prior naming
- `/venues/terracottage-ochre` is correct; leave it

Source is the venue record in Supabase, not the repo. **Resolve venue IDs live — do not trust remembered 15/16/17** (`venue-ids`).
*Exit: curl all three pages; no placeholder string, correct property name in `<meta name="description">`.*

**A2. Repoint the legal-page canonicals.** `/terms` and `/cancellation` both declare `canonical → https://picnicstories.com/{terms,cancellation}.html`, which resolves through **3 redirect hops** to the real `https://www.picnicstories.com/{terms,cancellation}`.
*Exit: canonical target returns 200 with `num_redirects = 0`.*

**A3. Make `lastmod` reflect content change, not build time.** All 21 sitemap URLs currently carry `lastmod 2026-09-07` — the deploy date. This marks everything as freshly updated on every deploy and trains Google to ignore the field.
*Exit: two consecutive deploys with no content change leave `lastmod` unmoved.*

### Tier 2 — One judgment call.

**A4. Decide the partner-venue pages.** ~335 impressions at 0% CTR per 28 days; the Beige Cafe cluster alone is 11 query variants, 157 impressions, zero clicks — navigational intent for someone else's brand.
Recommendation: **keep them**, and add an explicit rule excluding partner-venue queries from every SEO number. Noindexing saves nothing real and loses the occasional pivot. What matters is that it becomes a decision instead of a silent distortion in every dashboard.

### Tier 3 — Decide, don't drift.

**A5. Jaipur.** `/picnic-venues-jaipur` is at position 39.6 on 44 impressions. Either commit — **GBP verification first, not content** — or drop the page from the sitemap. Deferred twice already; half-built is the worst of the three states.

## 5. Explicitly NOT in this plan

**Adding internal links to the TerraCottage pages.** An earlier draft led with this. Verification killed it: all three are already server-rendered in the homepage venue grid and present in the sitemap. The 07-15 grid fix covers them equally. Their low impressions are a demand fact — Beige Cafe pulls 237 impressions because people search *"beige cafe leopard trail"* by name, and nobody searches "terracottage".

**Rewriting venue titles or expanding the 582-word venue template.** All twelve venue pages are near-identical in structure and length, which looks like a thin-content problem worth fixing. The demand evidence says a better-differentiated page still earns a handful of impressions. Revisit only if A1–A3 move the numbers.

**Any new content.** No new blog posts, no occasion landing pages, no city pages, until the scoreboard number moves.

## 6. Guardrails

- A1 is a **database** change; A2 and A3 are **repo** changes. Do not mix them in one commit.
- **No commits without explicit go-ahead** (`feedback-no-commit-without-permission`).
- Never `git add .` — ~5.2GB untracked media (`repo-hygiene-and-drift`).
- Any template touch requires a post-deploy raw-HTML check. The SPA shell means a broken prerender de-indexes silently (`SEO_GROWTH_PLAN.md`).

## 7. The risk that threatens this whole plan

**A click is not a booking here.** 44 of 46 confirmed bookings never touched the site (`meta-ads-attribution-gap`). Even a successful SEO outcome has no demonstrated path to revenue on current evidence. Do not justify any future content investment on click growth alone — require the PostHog organic→enquiry insight to be live first.

This is also the strongest argument for keeping Phase A small and stopping there.
