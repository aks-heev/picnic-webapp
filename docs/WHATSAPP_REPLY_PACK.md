Copy-paste scripts for WhatsApp Business Quick Replies (Settings → Business tools → Quick replies).
Shortcut names suggested per script. `[square brackets]` = fill in before sending.

**Venue pricing (confirmed from live site, 2026-07-06):**

| Venue | City | Base (The Setting) | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beige Cafe, Leopard Trail | Gurugram | **₹8,900** | 2–30 guests |
| The Sunroom, Palam Farms | Delhi | **₹13,900** | 2–10 guests |

⚠️ **Rule: never quote a package price before knowing the venue/city.** The two venues differ by ₹5,000 — anchoring a Delhi lead at ₹8,900 creates price shock at quote time. Scripts below always ask city first or show both prices.

> 2️⃣ Which date & how many guests?

### /food (meals question — never a flat "no")

>
> Most couples add it so everything's handled when they arrive 🍽️
>
> Want me to include it in your quote?

**Rule: quote the food add-on as "+₹X" on top of their package — never as a standalone total (₹9,900 was quoted as both a total and an add-on today; at Sunroom that number is wrong either way).**

### /price2 (price objection: "we're only 2 people")
> Totally fair question! The price covers the full private setup — styling, decor, exclusive spot, and our team setting up & packing down — so it's the same experience whether 2 or 6 join 🌿
>
> That said — if you share your budget, I'll tell you honestly what we can do for it. What range are you thinking?

**Rule: never end a price conversation without asking a question back.**

## Stage 5 — Follow-ups (fire on EVERY silent lead — this is where the money is)

### /fu1 (3 hours after packages/quote, no reply)
> Hi [name]! Did the packages make sense? 😊
> Happy to suggest the best fit for your [occasion] — or tweak anything to your budget 🧺

### /fu2 (24 hours, no reply — social proof)
> [Send 2–3 photos or a reel of a recent similar setup]
>
> Here's a [occasion] we styled at [venue] just last weekend, [name] 🤍 This is roughly what yours would look like. Want me to check if [date] is still open?

### /fu3 (72 hours, final nudge)
> Hi [name] — last check from my side 😊 [Date] slots at [venue] are filling up for the weekend.
>
> If the plan's still on, I can hold your slot with just ₹[X]. If the date changed, tell me the new one and I'll sort it. Either way — no pressure! 🌿

**Rule: label every quoted lead "Follow-up" immediately after sending the quote. The follow-up queue = that label, checked twice daily.**

├─ Button: "📅 Check availability" → HUMAN HANDOFF (assigned, labeled "Hot")
